# Playing

This page covers your first hour: the controls, the tutorial and the opening
main-story quests. It also lists the [magic words](magic-words.md) that help
when you're testing a particular part.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys or WASD | Move and turn |
| `R` | Auto-run (any movement key stops it) |
| `Ctrl` | Draw your weapon or attack |
| Mouse | Look around, click NPCs and objects |
| `Tab` | Sea chest: inventory, quests, skills and more |
| `` ` `` | SpeedChat menu |
| `Enter` | Type a chat message, or a `~magic word` |
| `Esc` | Main menu (options, log out, quit) |
| `F9` / `Shift+F9` | Take a screenshot / open the screenshot viewer |
| `F12` | Hide or show the interface |
| `Shift+F2` | Log out |

A ray of light shows where to go next for your active quest. If you lose track,
open the Quests page in your sea chest and make the quest active.

## The tutorial

Each new pirate plays the tutorial once. Doors lock behind you as you go, so
there's no wandering back.

1. **Make a pirate.** Pick your pirate's look and name. Make-A-Pirate carries
   on into the start of the tutorial. If you leave the game before it's
   finished, the half-made pirate is removed from your account, so you don't end
   up with a default pirate in your slot.
2. **Jail on Rambleshack.** You start in the jail. Make your way out.
3. **Grab your sea chest.** Doggerel Dan, the island's bartender, has it.
4. **Catch a ship.** Find Captain Bo Beck at the docks and board his boat.
5. **Cannon practice.** An undead ship sails up alongside, to the left of the
   ship already anchored there. Man the cannon and sink it.
6. **The Jolly Roger.** Once the undead ship goes down, Jolly Roger shows up and
   a cutscene plays. Jump off the boat when it's over and you'll arrive in Port
   Royal.

Only one pirate can be in the tutorial at a time on a server. Anyone else waits
in a queue for up to two minutes; if it's still busy after that, they get a
connection error and can try again.

Want to skip it while developing? See [configuration](configuration.md#the-tutorial).

## The opening quests in Port Royal

These come in order, and each one is handed to you when the one before it is
done:

| Quest | What to do |
|---|---|
| Arm Yourself | Get a cutlass from Will Turner in the warehouse near the shore. He teaches you your first cutlass moves. |
| Defeat A Skeleton | Defeat one skeleton with your new cutlass. |
| Find Tia Dalma | Find Tia Dalma in the jungle. She gives you a compass. |
| Recover Release Orders | Take the Black Pearl's impound release orders from the Navy soldiers near the Governor's mansion. |
| Get Governor's Stamp | Take the orders to the Governor's mansion to be stamped. |
| Visit Darby Drydock | The shipwright gives you your first ship. A name panel opens: pick or type a name and wait while he builds it. |
| Launch Your Ship | Launch your new ship from a dinghy at the docks. |
| Find Captain Barbossa | Sail to the cave on Devil's Anvil. He gives you a pistol. |
| Find Jack Sparrow | Jack is in the Faithful Bride tavern on Tortuga. |

After that the story branches into Chapter 3, and the side quests, crews,
guilds, treasure maps and parlor games open up.

## Ships

- You get your first ship, a light sloop, from Darby Drydock. Finishing his
  quest also lets the shipwrights sell you more ships. Until then they turn
  purchases down with "Trade timeout!".
- Shipwrights also repair your ships and buy them back.
- To sail, walk up to a dinghy at the docks and launch a ship. It spawns at sea
  next to the island. Take the wheel to sail, or man a cannon to fight.

## Dying

When your health runs out on land you're thrown in jail. Kick the cell door
open to get out.

## Useful magic words while playing

| Goal | Command |
|---|---|
| Skip the main-story step you're on | `~skipQuest` |
| Get around faster | `~run`, `~tp Tortuga`, `~tp Port Royal` |
| Stop dying | `~god` |
| Money for ships and gear | `~gold 50000` |

The full list is in [magic words](magic-words.md).
